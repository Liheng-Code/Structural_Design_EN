None of these four images shows the top-propped basement wall. They show other kinds of earth-
retaining structure kept in the same folder. Use them only to compare structural systems. Never
take dimensions, materials or reinforcement from them for the basement wall.

## Image 1 — Sheet pile with RC capping beam, tie-rod anchored (8OX1a1puc_uBka4Gu3qm4z1.jfif)
Section A-A of a waterfront wall: steel sheet piles FSP IIIA (400 × 150 × 13.1) embedded in an
800 mm wide × 1500 mm deep RC capping beam, land side on the left and sea side on the right.
Shown on the drawing:
- Concrete G35 with 7.5 % silica fume, cover 60 mm.
- Main reinforcement 25-T16; trimmer bars 4-T16; links T12 @ 200 mm; shear hooks T12 @ 1000 mm
  through 20 mm holes in the pile.
- Anchorage by 32 mm tie rods, grade 500 MPa, with turnbuckles @ 2400 mm c/c.
Structural system: anchored (tied-back) embedded wall. It differs from the basement wall, which
is propped at the top by a floor slab.

## Image 2 — Cantilever RC retaining wall, reinforcement exercise (BwVzn-oyQsY6tPrYdedN.jfif)
A textbook task: "Prepare the reinforcement drawings for the R.C. Retaining Wall shown in
Figure 2."
- Free-standing cantilever wall: stem about 2250 mm high above a base 625 mm deep (275 mm slab
  plus a downstand key about 300 mm wide). Toe 900 mm, heel 1400 mm.
- Bars: Y12 @ 150, R10 @ 250 / 300, F82 mesh, 4/Y16 in the key, construction joint (CJ) at the
  stem base.
- Wall length 5500 mm, cover 40 mm, concrete strength 20 MPa.
Structural system: unpropped cantilever with its own footing. It behaves like the basement wall's
**construction stage** only, before the ground-floor slab prop exists.

## Image 3 — Anchored sheet-pile quay wall with anchor wall (NktMIIBqEX2ZG4y5Jw8hUoh.jfif)
Quay wall with these parts:
- Front wall: AZ 50 sheet piles, L = 21.0 m, S 430 GP, toe at −19.00.
- Anchor wall: AZ 20-700, L = 9.0 m, S 390 GP, 29.50 m behind the front wall.
- Connection: Ø62 mm tie rod at +1.40.
- Levels: deck +4.00 with gravel; water +1.20 (MSL); dredged bed −10.00 with quarry stone to
  −11.00.
- Detail circles: waling and tie-rod connections, and a turnbuckle.
Structural system: single-anchored embedded wall. It is a marine structure, not a building
basement.

## Image 4 — Secant pile wall with shotcrete facing (eYF5mE9ZChqAshM4Y8oSI0nzYv0HGJU.jfif)
Plan detail of a secant pile wall: reinforced (male) and unreinforced (female) piles overlapping.
- Facing: shotcrete with mesh reinforcement on the air face.
- Connection: starter bars drilled and epoxied into the piles.
- A membrane between the piles and the shotcrete facing.
Structural system: an embedded wall that can form a basement perimeter where there's no room for
an open excavation. It is an alternative to a cast-in-place propped wall, and is not modelled in
the basement wall plan.
